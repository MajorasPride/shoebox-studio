# Shoebox Studio

**Cut, Paste, Play.**

An arts-and-crafts game maker by MajorasPride. Make your own pieces at the School Desk out of paper, clay, yarn and cardboard, build platformer levels with them, then play them. It runs in the browser on phone and PC.

**Play it:** https://majoraspride.github.io/shoebox-studio/

Formerly called Craft Loft. Saves made under that name still load.

## Family
- [Beat Bop Doodle](https://majoraspride.github.io/beat-bop-doodle/), a Shoebox Studio game. Play it on the retro console in your Little Town house. Story Shelf opens from the bookshelf in the studio.
