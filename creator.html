1. Pieces

Make a piece in the game (School Desk, Pixel UI Kit, Toy Box), then pick it here. Pieces come from the Toy Box in this browser, or from a Shoebox file you import.

2. Make a new thing

Tap a piece on the left to use it.

The building gets a door and a room with a counter. Shop stock you add for it shows on the counter.

The picture above is the finished picture. Each step can have its own picture: tap "Use picked piece" on a step after picking a piece.

3. The content pack

Everything here ships to every new player once the pack is published. Export it, put content-pack.json in the Shoebox Drive folder and write "content pack ready" in the Inbox. Your work is kept in this browser as you go.
